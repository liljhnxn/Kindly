---

## Slide 2: The Problem
- **Predatory Middlemen:** Web2 gig platforms take 10%–25% cuts from small tasks, making casual assistance financially unviable.
- **Fragmented Social Channels:** Asking for help on Discord, Reddit, or Telegram leads to spam, ghosting, and lost accountability.
- **Speculative Web3 Overload:** The crypto space is dominated by speculative DeFi and paid NFTs, leaving social and civic utility underserved.
- **Lack of Trust & Verifiability:** In conventional forums, it's impossible to verify who actually showed up to assist.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution — Kindly
- **Direct On-Chain Mutual Aid:** Anyone can post a small task (Tech, Education, Design, Personal Assistance, Community) and community members volunteer.
- **Zero Middlemen, Zero Fees:** No tokens, no platform cuts, and no payment barriers.
- **Smart Contract Accountability:** Immutable lifecycle tracking: `Open` ➔ `InProgress` ➔ `Completed`.
- **Human-Centric Design:** Elegant, calming aesthetic (Ivory & Forest Green) that welcomes everyday users rather than traders.

---

## Slide 4: How It Works (The 3-Step Flow)
1. **Request:** A user connects MetaMask to Botchain Testnet and submits a small request with title, category, and description.
2. **Volunteer:** A community member spots an open request and clicks "Volunteer to Help", committing on-chain to assist.
3. **Complete:** Once the request is fulfilled, the original requester clicks "Mark as Completed", recording the act of kindness forever.

---

## Slide 5: Core Value Proposition & Rules
- **Anti-Exploit Design:**
  - Users cannot volunteer for their own requests (prevents self-farming).
  - Only one volunteer assigned per request (prevents duplicate collisions).
  - Only the original requester can confirm completion or cancel.
  - Cancelled requests cannot be claimed.
- **Verifiable Reputation Foundation:** Creates an immutable on-chain footprint of real-world helpfulness.

---

## Slide 6: Product & UX Highlights
- **Exactly 1 Webpage & 1 Smart Contract:** No database vulnerabilities, zero private servers, and no central points of failure.

- **Interactive Filtering:** Instant categorization (Education, Technology, Design, Personal Assistance, Community, Other) and status filtering.
